Expert-led care

beyond the purchase

Buying the boat is the easiest part, so we take pride in guiding how you care for and upkeep your acquisition. From the first viewing to your fifth summer at sea, we'll make sure you're confident every step of the way so you can enjoy your time out at sea.

local_shipping

Delivery

Your vessel arrives on the water, not on a schedule. We handle transport and launch anywhere in Maltese waters.

tune

Setup

Engine run-in, electronics paired, trim calibrated, tender kit aboard. On our RIBs, the outboard can be specified or changed to whichever brand you prefer. You step on to a boat that is already yours.

cleaning_services

Upkeep

Wash-downs, antifoul, tube and upholstery care, winter cover. The details that keep a hull looking new in its fifth season.

build

Maintenance

Servicing to Suzuki and Yamaha intervals, logged and stamped, so nothing about your vessel's condition is ever guessed at.

Technical support

One number, answered by the people who commissioned your boat. Diagnostics on the pontoon, not in a ticket queue.

Lifetime care

For as long as the vessel is yours: priority workshop slots, parts held on the island, and a record of every hour she has run.

JUST IN CASE YOU'RE WONDERING

Questions

A licence is required for both vessel classes. We walk you through the current Transport Malta requirements for the craft you have chosen and point you to a recognised course, well before handover day.

Boats in stock can be handed over within a few days of purchase. Custom builds take around three to four months to complete with installations, so to have yours for the coming season, order by February. We confirm the delivery window in writing before any deposit is taken.

Tube colour, gelcoat, upholstery, teak, canvas and electronics are all specified with you before the build slot is confirmed. The standard finishes listed under each model are a starting point, not a limit.
